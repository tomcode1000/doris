How it works

Four steps, and the scammer never meets your family.

  1. 01

    Forward

    Send any suspicious email to ask-doris@agentmail.to. Doris tells you plainly whether it's a scam.

  2. 02

    Respond

    She writes to the scammer from her own address, doris.pemberton@agentmail.to. Your address never appears.

  3. 03

    Distract

    Rambling, polite, endless questions about who, what and which relative. Every hour on Doris is an hour off someone real.

  4. 04

    Record

    Links are opened in a throwaway computer wiped after every visit. What the trap wanted is logged, and phishing sites are reported.

Her rules

What Doris will never do.

These are enforced in code, not left to the AI. Each rule has an automated test.

  1. 1
    Never sends money, card or bank numbers.Any reply containing a card-like or bank account number is refused.
  2. 2
    Never mentions the family.The forwarder's name and address are removed before Doris reads the scam.
  3. 3
    Never threatens anyone.She stays polite, chatty and patient.
  4. 4
    Never runs anything a scammer sends.Downloads are recorded, never opened.
