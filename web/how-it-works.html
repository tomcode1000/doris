How Doris works

One scam's journey, where the keys live, and the automated tests that enforce her rules.

One scam's journey

  1. 01

    Forward

    A family forwards a suspected scam to ask-doris@agentmail.to.

  2. 02

    Judge

    Doris decides whether it is a scam and tells the family in plain words.

  3. 03

    Reply

    She writes to the scammer from her own address, never the family's.

  4. 04

    Open

    Any link is opened in a separate throwaway computer.

  5. 05

    Record

    What the page asked for, and where it really led, is recorded.

  6. 06

    Wipe

    The throwaway computer rewinds itself to a clean snapshot.

  7. 07

    Report

    Confirmed phishing pages are reported for takedown, and everything goes into the public log.

Where the keys live

Two computers, and neither holds a key.

In a vault outside both

Email, the AI model and storage are reached through Fly.io Sprites connectors. The keys stay in Fly; no key exists inside either computer.

Doris's home

Reads mail, decides and writes letters. Only this computer is allowed to use her connectors.

The throwaway computer

Opens scammers' links in a browser. Every connector refuses it, and it is wiped after every visit. If a page turns nasty, there is nothing there to steal.

Her rules

What Doris will never do.

Each rule is code, checked by a named test that runs on every change.

  1. 1
    Never sends money, card or bank numbers.Replies with card-like or bank account numbers are refused.tests/test_rules.py::test_outgoing_blocks
  2. 2
    Never mentions the family.The forwarder is stripped from the scam and can never reach the scammer or the diary.tests/test_brain.py::test_family_identity_never_reaches_scammer_or_diary
  3. 3
    Never threatens anyone.Threatening language blocks a reply.tests/test_rules.py::test_outgoing_blocks
  4. 4
    Never runs anything a scammer sends.Downloads and unsafe addresses never reach the throwaway computer.tests/test_brain.py::test_trapdoor_refuses_unsafe_links_without_calling
  5. 5
    Types fake details only into the scam's own page.Never into a real site the scam redirects to.tests/test_rules.py::test_may_fill_canary
  6. 6
    Never writes to government, university or no-reply addresses.A spoofed sender is not a reason to bother a real institution.tests/test_rules.py::test_reply_targets
